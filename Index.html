About

A watch is made up of several key parts that work together to keep time and protect the mechanism. The **case** houses all the internal components and is usually made of metal, while the **crystal** (glass) covers the dial and protects it from damage. Inside, the **movement** is the engine of the watch, controlling how time is measured and displayed. The **dial** shows the time, with **hands** or digital numbers indicating hours, minutes, and sometimes seconds. On the outside, the **crown** is used to set the time and date, and the **strap or bracelet** secures the watch to the wrist. Each part plays an important role in both the function and appearance of the watch.
